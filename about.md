# Skill Issue Roaster

Every time you die, you get a brand new passive-aggressive roast.

## Features
- 20 roasted one-liners, rotated on every death
- Shows your death percent in several of the roasts
- Completion message when you (unexpectedly) beat a level
- Disable anytime in the mod's settings
